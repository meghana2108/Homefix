| D14 | 2026-11-19 | Full brain is `qwen3-vl:8b-instruct`, not `qwen3-vl:8b` | `ollama show qwen3-vl:8b` lists "thinking" with default on, same problem as D11. The instruct tag lists no thinking; `go_no_go.py --profile full` prints GO with it |
| D15 | 2026-11-20 | The repair request is never offered by default. Escalate to your landlord" link; a plain question ("Is it heating or cooling?", "Can I have a cat?") gets nothing, as does an answer still coming, after an error, when HomeFix asked back, or for code (`escalation()` in `frontend/src/state/evidence.ts`) | Most questions are just questions. Escalating is the renter's choice and only makes sense for a problem; HomeFix points it out only when the answer itself says this needs someone else |
| D16 | 2026-11-20 | Safety principle: fixed rules have the final say; AI can only make HomeFix more careful (add a stop, a question back, a second look), never less | The 2B model can't tell when it's guessing (it answered "ASK:" for every photo), so models perceive and compare meanings and our code decides. See docs/SAFETY.md |
| D17 | 2026-11-20 | A meaning-based danger net (bge-small vs hazard and safe examples, `config/safety_semantic.yaml`) runs after the regex on questions and voice-note transcripts | Held-out: regex alone 12/25 dangerous phrasings caught, with the net 24/25, 0/30 false stops; 0/100 false stops on practice pack questions |
| D18 | 2026-11-20 | Vague or safety questions about a photo get two focused danger looks first; either naming a sign stops; descriptions are scanned for observed danger | One cold look missed 3 of 4 real hazard photos; two looks caught 4/4 and stopped 0 of 6 safe photos |
| D19 | 2026-11-20 | A display a specific question depends on is read twice (two wordings); conflicting numbers get a question back | The model samples at temperature 1; on an unreadable digit its reads differ (390/391/598/389), and guessing one is a confident wrong answer |
| D20 | 2026-11-20 | A sound with an alarm label that isn't confidently first asks "is anything like a kettle on?"; "no" or "not sure" stops | A real kettle came back "carbon monoxide alarm 18%"; real alarms score far below the 0.5 stop threshold too |
| D21 | 2026-11-20 | "Is this a problem?" and "is this vague?" are the word rule OR meaning (`config/intent_examples.yaml`); the API sends `intent` on `final` | Held-out: problem 4/10 → 10/10 (1/10 wrong), vague 2/6 → 6/6 (0/6 wrong) |
